    About

    Mtveri FM is an independent electronic music radio based in Tbilisi, with live broadcasts and DJ sessions. Erekle, its founder, is a music selector and record collector from Tbilisi, Georgia.

    His connection with music started in early childhood, listening to his favorite rock bands on a reel-to-reel tape recorder, which shaped his listening culture. He was introduced to electronic music in 2000, when a friend gave him the Matt Darey Pure Euphoria CD compilation. After listening to it, he realized this music was for him, and a new stage of musical development began.

    He got into the golden age of progressive house and trance during the 2000s, listened to many radio shows, went to clubs and critically evaluated DJ mixes. In 2004, Âme’s Mifune / Shiro EP and their self-titled album, released on Sonar Kollektiv, became his first steps into house and deep house.

    This continued for years as a listener, collector and audiophile, but he lived, felt and searched like a DJ. After regularly playing at closed parties for friends, he recorded his first mix, Groove Mix 01, and uploaded it to Mixcloud. Today he is the author of the radio shows Vinyl Sessions, Spotify Weekly and Groove Mix Sessions.
